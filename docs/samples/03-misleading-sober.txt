A hot drink made from lemon and honey can cure influenza within a single day, according to a study published by a regional health institute this week. The researchers said that patients who drank the mixture three times daily recovered faster than those who took no treatment, and that the drink removed the need for vaccination against the virus.

"Our results show a clear and rapid recovery, and we believe that annual flu vaccines are no longer necessary for healthy adults," said the study's lead author, who works at the institute. The report said that 120 volunteers took part and that nearly all of them reported feeling better after 24 hours.

A spokesperson for the institute said the findings would be shared with local clinics later this month. The study has not yet been reviewed by outside experts, and the institute did not say how the volunteers were selected or whether the symptoms of the group that received no treatment were compared over the same period. Several pharmacies in the area have already begun promoting the drink to customers as an alternative to seasonal vaccination.
