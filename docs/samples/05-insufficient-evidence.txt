The city council voted 6 to 3 on Monday night to approve a rezoning proposal that would allow a four-storey apartment building on a former car dealership site at the corner of Maple and Fifth. The developer said the project would include 96 apartments, a small ground-floor cafe and underground parking, and that construction could begin next spring if permits are granted.

Several residents spoke against the plan during the public hearing, saying the building would be taller than anything nearby and would add to traffic on Maple Street. Councillor David Ortiz, who supported the proposal, said the city needs more housing and that the developer had agreed to set aside ten percent of the units at reduced rents.

Councillor Priya Nair, who voted against it, said she was concerned about parking and about the loss of local businesses. The mayor said a traffic study would be carried out before final approval, and that the council would review the results in the autumn. The next council meeting will be held on the first Monday of next month at city hall.
